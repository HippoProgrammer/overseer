# Privacy Policy
## Storage
### User Data
- We may store the Discord IDs, NationStates nation names, and activity levels across NationStates and Discord, of any users within guilds with the bot installed.
### Guild Data
- We may store Discord IDs of channels and roles that have been specifically configured.
## Usage
- We will never share any of this data with any third-party to the extent permitted under law.
## Deletion
- If you wish to have any of your data removed, please contact the person responsible for hosting that specific instance of Overseer.
